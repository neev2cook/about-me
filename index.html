🚀 The Big Achievement

At the World Robot Olympiad (WRO) Regionals, Team Holo Nexus (Neev Saraiya, Raghav Joshi, and Aarav Patel) proudly clinched 2nd Place in the Future Innovators category!

Representing Navrachana International School, the team developed cutting-edge technology that beautifully merged history with automated science.

🤖 Project Spotlight: Roboguide

Roboguide is an AI-powered automated tour guide built for museums and historic landmarks. Designed under the international WRO theme "Robots Meet Culture," it changes how visitors explore historical sites:

  • Pepper's Ghost Hologram: Projects a live 3D conversational avatar right in front of you.
  • Smart Navigation: Built-in ultrasonic sensors to detect when tourists approach.
  • Multilingual Support: Instantly breaks down language barriers for global tourists.
